Hindustan Unilever, summer 2024

Into growth

My first campaign, and why some messages spread.

The summer after my second year, I joined Novology, a skincare brand at Hindustan Unilever, India’s biggest consumer goods company. The brand was launching a new product line, and I got to own the influencer brief: what creators would say about it, and who they would say it to.

What I did

Before writing a single line, I spoke with 65 consumers and 12 dermatologists, and I built the brief on what they told me, in their own words. From Mumbai, the brief went out to more than fifteen creators across India, and each of them shared it with followers who already trusted them.

What happened

+20%engagement on the new line

In 2020, I couldn’t explain why one post worked and the next one didn’t. Four years later, I finally could. Engagement went up because the campaign said what customers needed to hear, in words they already used.

Bain & Company, 2025 to 2026

Bain OASIS

Seventy offices. One way to collect data.

At Bain & Company, I led the build of an AI-native portal. It gives more than seventy offices around the world one standard way to send in their data, then processes it all the way to the final numbers.

The problem

Every office collected its data its own way, so a team of eight had to chase all 70+ offices over long email chains, one office at a time, before any of the numbers could be used. A single cycle took three to four months.

The fix

My first version just moved the old steps online. That taught me to cut what nobody needs before you automate what’s left. Now every office submits data the same way, AI reads and checks the source documents, and the portal handles the processing from start to finish.

What changed

  • 50%savings in time
  • $80Ksaved every year
  • 70+offices on one standard
